Gift Ideas Down Under is a small, independent hobby blog written from Brisbane. It exists because "what do I get them?" is one of the most common questions in any Australian group chat from November onwards, and most gift lists online are written for American prices, American seasons and American postage.

Here you'll find gift guides sorted by **occasion** (Kris Kringle, Secret Santa, teacher gifts, birthdays, Mother's Day and Father's Day) and **budget in Australian dollars**. There's a lean towards personalised gifts, because a name, a date or a small in-joke is often what turns a cheap present into one people keep.

## How ideas are chosen

- **Realistic budgets.** Price ranges are rough AUD figures for what things typically cost from Australian shops and online sellers at the time of writing. They move around, so treat them as a guide.
- **Australian conditions.** A Christmas in 35-degree heat changes what makes a good gift. Chocolate melts on the porch, candles soften in hot cars, and beach and backyard gear gets used.
- **Delivery timing.** Personalised items take longer because someone has to make them. Where it matters, posts point you to the official Australia Post cut-off dates rather than guessing.
- **No fake reviews or made-up statistics.** If I haven't been able to check something, I'll say so.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog, not a shop. I enjoy making things, and now and then I may feature something I've made myself. When that happens, I'll say so clearly in the post, right next to the item, and it will be judged on the same terms as everything else. There are no paid placements or sponsored posts here.
